How we deploy

Live in weeks, not quarters.

A bounded product process with a defined end, not an implementation programme that expands to fill the year.

Week 1

Access and mapping

We connect to your systems and map how the work actually moves today, including the parts that were never written down.

Weeks 2–3

First process live

One process, running alongside your team so you can compare its output against what they would have produced. Nothing posts until you approve the rules.

Week 4 onward

Handover and expansion

Your team stops doing the process. We stay on as the domain team, tuning logic and adding the next one.

Deployments are led by chartered accountants and forward-deployed engineers who encode your specific treatments and stay on afterwards. Not to do the work. To make sure it's done correctly.
